What We Teach

Subjects built for exam readiness

Each subject follows the NERDC curriculum and prepares students for Common Entrance and secondary school transition.

✄️

Mathematics

From number recognition in P1 to algebra and geometry prep for secondary school in P5. We build real fluency, not just memorisation.

P1: Numeracy Foundations P3: Times Tables P5: Algebra & Percentages Exam Readiness
📚

English Language

From phonics and sight words in P1 to essay writing and advanced comprehension in P5. Critical for all Common Entrance exams.

P1: Phonics & Literacy P3: Grammar & Tenses P5: Essay Writing Common Entrance Prep
🔬

Basic Science

Hands-on concepts covering living things, states of matter, body systems, and environmental science across all grade levels.

P1–P2: Living Things P3–P4: Matter & Forces P5: Ecology

Local Languages

Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa — keeping children connected to their heritage while building literacy in their mother tongue.

Yoruba Igbo Hausa Beginner & Intermediate
