Part 1
Mental Maths and Fluency

No calculator. Target: under 5 minutes for all four.

London / Lagos

A bus leaves the station with 35 passengers. It drops off 14 at the first stop, then picks up 8 more. How many passengers are on the bus now?

35 - 14 + 8 = ?
Answer
29 passengers
The Kitchen

A box contains 24 cubes of seasoning. If your family uses 3 cubes each day, how many days will the box last?

24 / 3 = ?
Answer
8 days
Ankara Fabric

A tailor needs 4 metres of fabric for each child's outfit. How many metres are needed to make 6 identical outfits?

4 x 6 = ?

Chike's Savings

(5 x 7) - 12 = ?

Part 2
Word Problems and Multi-Step Logic

Ask your child to show their working. These test the kind of reasoning that sets strong performers apart across international curricula.

A
The Party Jollof Rice - Fractions and Division

Mummy makes a large tray of party Jollof rice, divided into 20 equal portions, for a family gathering.

  • 1.Cousin Tunde eats 1/4 of all the portions. How many portions did he eat?
  • 2.Aunty Funmi takes 5 portions home for her household.
  • 3.What fraction of the original 20 portions is left on the tray?
Answer
1/2 of the tray (10 portions remaining)
  • 1.1/4 x 20 = 5 portions eaten by Tunde
  • 2.Aunty Funmi takes 5. Total removed: 5 + 5 = 10
  • 3.Remaining: 20 - 10 = 10 portions = 10/20 = 1/2
B
The Weekend Market - Multi-Step Budget

You visit the local African grocery store with a budget of $50.

  • 1.You buy 2 bags of plantain chips at $4.50 each.
  • 2.You buy a bag of garri for $8.25.
  • 3.You spend the rest of your money on frozen fish.

How much did you spend on the frozen fish?

Answer
$32.75
  • 1.Plantain chips: 2 x $4.50 = $9.00
  • 2.Garri: $8.25. Total so far: $9.00 + $8.25 = $17.25
  • 3.Fish: $50.00 - $17.25 = $32.75
