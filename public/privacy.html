Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ThinkViva ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it. By using our website and services at thinkviva.org, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Booking enquiries: parent name, email address, phone number, child's name, grade, subjects, country, and any message you include.
  • Tutor applications: full name, email, phone, location, qualifications, experience, subjects, availability, CV/résumé, and IT readiness test responses.
  • Feedback submissions: name, email, role, star rating, and written comments.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: email address only.

We do not currently collect payment card information directly. Any future payment processing will be handled by a PCI-compliant third-party provider (e.g. Paystack).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to booking enquiries and match children with suitable tutors.
  • To process and review tutor applications.
  • To send booking confirmations and service-related email communications.
  • To improve our website and services based on feedback.
  • To send occasional learning tips and updates if you have subscribed to our newsletter (you may unsubscribe at any time).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in a cloud database hosted by MongoDB Atlas. Email communications are sent via SMTP using secure (TLS-encrypted) connections. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Vercel: website hosting and deployment (vercel.com)
  • MongoDB Atlas: cloud database (mongodb.com)
  • Email SMTP provider: for sending transactional emails

Each of these providers has their own privacy practices; we recommend reviewing their policies if you have specific concerns.

5. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies, primarily those necessary for the site to function (e.g. session security). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No analytics platform (such as Google Analytics) is currently active on this site.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR for users in the UK and EU) to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@thinkviva.org. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

We retain booking and application data for as long as needed to deliver our services and comply with legal obligations. Feedback and newsletter data is retained until you request its deletion. You may contact us at any time to have your records removed.

8. Children's Privacy

Our tutoring services are for children, but we collect information from parents and guardians only, not directly from children. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from anyone under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
