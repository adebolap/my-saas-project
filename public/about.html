Our Story

About ThinkViva

We focus on making learning engaging, interactive, and tailored to each child's pace and goals, whether they need help catching up, staying ahead, or preparing for exams.

Our Mission

Bridging learning gaps, one child at a time.

ThinkViva was built to solve a real problem: diaspora families and Nigerian households struggling to find qualified, reliable tutors who truly understand their child's curriculum and culture.

With experienced tutors, flexible scheduling, and a supportive online environment, we make high-quality education convenient and accessible for busy families, wherever they are in the world.
